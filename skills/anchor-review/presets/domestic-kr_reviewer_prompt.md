Rules:
- Read only the assigned files: {FILES}.
- Do not open any other folder or file, do not search the web. Write your complete review to `{OUT}` and make no other changes.
- Judge only from the text. Do not use prior knowledge, notes or memory about specific manuscripts or authors, and do not guess where any paper came from.

How to judge (important):
- Judge only these four things:
  - the size of the contribution
  - novelty
  - whether the main results are strong and actually support the central claim
  - how useful the work is to this conference's audience (Korean researchers and practitioners)
- Ignore these entirely. They earn no credit and no penalty:
  - writing polish, completeness, formatting, length or density, missing figures
  - statistical procedure (pre-registration, multiple-comparison correction, CIs, seeds)
  - reproducibility, how faithfully baselines were re-run, how candid the limitations section is
  - sheer experimental volume by itself
- A paper with a careful, large study whose proposed method does not win under realistic conditions should be scored on what it contributes, not on how carefully it reports.
- A small paper with sloppy reporting should be scored on its idea and headline results, unless an error actually invalidates the main claim.
